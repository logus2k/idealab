# AI Ideas Catalog

## Taxonomy (Tag Reference)

Every idea is tagged across six facets so the catalog can be filtered by area, technology, audience, value driver, or maturity. Tags use the format `facet/value` and are lowercase / kebab-case so they can be grep'd or parsed.

- **function/** — `sales`, `marketing`, `customer-service`, `contact-center`, `customer-insights`, `knowledge-management`, `productivity`, `data-analytics`, `enterprise-tech`, `cio`, `strategy`, `innovation`, `design`, `partnerships`, `governance`, `talent`, `hr`, `finance`, `legal`, `compliance`, `cybersecurity`, `software-engineering`, `supply-chain`, `manufacturing`, `procurement`, `randd`, `network-ops`
- **industry/** — `cross-industry`, `retail`, `cpg`, `banking`, `financial-services`, `wealth-management`, `private-equity`, `insurance`, `healthcare`, `pharma`, `biotech`, `manufacturing`, `industrials`, `aerospace`, `logistics`, `telecom`, `energy`, `utilities`, `oil-gas`, `media`, `entertainment`, `education`, `legal-tech`, `professional-services`
- **tech/** — `genai`, `llm`, `rag`, `agentic`, `copilot`, `chatbot`, `classical-ml`, `predictive-ml`, `nlp`, `computer-vision`, `multimodal`, `voice-ai`, `text-to-image`, `text-to-video`, `gan`, `fine-tuning`, `generative-design`, `synthetic-data`, `vector-search`, `digital-twin`
- **audience/** — `customer-facing`, `employee-facing`, `partner-facing`, `back-office`
- **value/** — `revenue`, `cost-reduction`, `productivity`, `risk-reduction`, `compliance`, `customer-experience`, `time-to-market`, `quality`, `decision-support`, `innovation`, `talent`
- **maturity/** — `proven` (deployed with measured outcomes), `emerging` (early adopters / mixed results), `pattern` (cross-cutting capability / framework, not a single deployment)

   - When AI is central to ~25% of an organization's work, transformation is well underway; under that, there is white space. *Source: Bain & Company press release, Dec 10 2024 ([link](https://www.bain.com/about/media-center/press-releases/2024/bain--company-enhances-leadership-of-its-digital-practicesai-insights-and-solutions-and-enterprise-technologyamid-high-client-demand)).*
   - McKinsey estimates generative AI could add $2.6T–$4.4T annually across 63 use cases in 16 business functions; ~75% of the value concentrates in customer operations, marketing & sales, software engineering, and R&D. *Source: McKinsey "Economic potential of generative AI" ([link](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/tech-and-ai/our-insights/the-economic-potential-of-generative-ai-the-next-productivity-frontier)).*
   - Industry baselines: 88% of organizations use AI in 2025 (vs. 50% in 2022); the share *only piloting* GenAI fell from 39% (2024) to 13% (2025) — the market has moved from pilots to scaled deployment. *Source: itransition "AI Use Cases and Trends 2026" ([link](https://www.itransition.com/ai/use-cases)).*
4. **Sequencing** — Most engagements blend (a) a flagship internal platform (Sage-style), (b) an employee enablement layer (Copilots + custom-agent marketplace), and (c) 2–3 client-facing flagship use cases (chatbot, content gen, call insights).
5. **Partnership angle** — For any idea, identify which ecosystem partner (hyperscaler, foundation-model lab, SaaS vendor) accelerates delivery vs. building in-house.
6. **Governance** — Every idea above should be paired with a Responsible AI checkpoint before deployment; high-stakes domains (insurance, healthcare, financial services) require explicit human-in-the-loop oversight given growing regulatory scrutiny. *Source: Bain & Company press release, Dec 10 2024 ([link](https://www.bain.com/about/media-center/press-releases/2024/bain--company-enhances-leadership-of-its-digital-practicesai-insights-and-solutions-and-enterprise-technologyamid-high-client-demand)); Stanford Report Jan 2026 ([link](https://news.stanford.edu/stories/2026/01/ai-algorithms-health-insurance-care-risks-research)).*
