# McKinsey — Rewired (Digital & AI Transformation)

## 1. Executive Snapshot
"Rewired" is McKinsey's book-length playbook for digital and AI transformation, built from work with 200+ large companies. It defines **six capabilities** every enterprise must build in parallel and treats transformation as a permanent operating posture, not a project. Refreshed extensions include the **AI Transformation Manifesto (12 themes)** and **Rewired 2.0** (talent-specific update for the generative-AI era).

## 2. Context & Strategic Drivers
McKinsey's premise: digital and AI value increasingly concentrates in firms that have rewired how they operate. PoCs without operating-model change deliver no compounding ROI; cross-functional C-suite collaboration is the only way to escape pilot purgatory. The framework targets CEOs and CXOs as the accountable owners.

## 3. Objectives & Target Outcomes
- Move organizations from isolated pilots to enterprise-wide capability.
- Build a baseline of competence across all six capabilities simultaneously.
- Sustain a continuous improvement journey rather than a one-time program.
- Outcompete on the back of compounding tech and data leverage.

## 4. Adoption Roadmap / Phases
The **six capabilities** the framework requires (each treated as a workstream that must be built and continuously matured):
1. **Transformation roadmap** that targets real value (domain-by-domain value at stake).
2. **Talent bench** of skilled experts (digital and AI roles, in-house).
3. **Operating model** that allows the firm to move at pace (agile, product-and-platform).
4. **Distributed, flexible technology environment** (modern data, modular architecture).
5. **Data embedded** throughout the organization (data products, governed reuse).
6. **Adoption and scaling** mechanics that ensure solutions realize meaningful gains.

Extensions: the **AI Transformation Manifesto** layers in 12 themes (agents, foundation-model strategy, data foundations for AI, talent reskilling, governance, etc.); **Rewired 2.0** adds a specific generative-AI talent plan.

## 5. Training & Workforce Enablement
- Build an internal "talent bench" — McKinsey advocates hiring and developing scarce technical talent in-house rather than outsourcing the core.
- Universal AI fluency programs alongside deep expert tracks.
- Rewired 2.0 explicitly addresses workforce reshape in the GenAI era (role redesign, prompt fluency, change-management depth).

## 6. Product Ideation & Development
- Domain-led "rewiring" by business unit, with value-at-stake quantified upfront.
- Pair business and tech leaders to translate use cases into product backlogs.
- Treat AI/data products as durable assets (owned, versioned, instrumented), not one-off engagements.

## 7. Cost Reduction Levers
- Process re-engineering using AI (automation + augmentation across the customer / employee journey).
- Tech modernization to reduce duplicated infrastructure and integration debt.
- Data-product reuse to cut analytics rework.
- Operating-model shift to product/platform teams reduces project overhead.

## 8. Portfolio & Competitive Positioning
McKinsey argues that companies that build all six capabilities outcompete rivals on cost-to-serve, decision speed, and product-launch velocity. Sustained transformation creates a self-reinforcing advantage as data, talent, and modular tech compound over time.

## 9. Market Expansion Plays
- AI-enabled personalization and pricing to deepen share in served markets.
- AI-augmented sales coverage to expand into adjacent segments.
- Faster product launches and localization to enter new geographies.
- New AI-only propositions ("only possible in the AI era") for whitespace markets.

## 10. Technology & Architecture
- Modern distributed data platforms with data products and lineage.
- Modular, API-first architecture; cloud-native footprint.
- Foundation-model strategy that mixes frontier APIs, fine-tuned open models, and orchestration.
- MLOps / LLMOps platform for productionization.

## 11. Governance, Risk & Responsible AI
McKinsey treats responsible AI and governance as one of the cross-cutting capabilities. Recommended controls: an AI governance forum, model-risk policies, evaluation harnesses, human-in-the-loop for high-stakes decisions, and continuous monitoring.

## 12. KPIs & Measured Outcomes
McKinsey's QuantumBlack and State of AI reports cite outsized financial impact for "AI high performers." Reported (verify in current sources): high performers achieve materially higher revenue contribution from AI and faster EBITDA improvement than peers; specific numbers vary by report year.

## 13. Risks, Constraints & Open Questions
- Common failure modes: pilot purgatory, fragmented data, weak adoption.
- Risk of underinvesting in any one of the six capabilities (the framework is explicit that all six must move together).
- C-suite buy-in is the single largest gating factor.
