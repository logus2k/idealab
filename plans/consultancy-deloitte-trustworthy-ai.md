# Deloitte — State of GenAI + Trustworthy AI Framework

## 1. Executive Snapshot
Deloitte's adoption posture combines a quarterly **State of Generative AI in the Enterprise** survey-driven view (the latest cycle surveyed 3,235 leaders across 24 countries between August and September 2025) with the **Trustworthy AI Framework** — a multidimensional model around governance, ethics, resilience, privacy, security, legal / contractual obligations, and alignment with company values. The combination is targeted at boards and CXOs who need a benchmark plus an operating model.

## 2. Context & Strategic Drivers
Deloitte's read of 2025–2026: worker access to AI grew ~50% in 2025; the share of companies with ≥40% of AI projects in production is set to double within six months; but only **1 in 5** companies has a mature governance model for autonomous AI agents. The AI skills gap is the largest barrier, and education is the #1 talent adjustment companies are making.

## 3. Objectives & Target Outcomes
- Benchmark adoption maturity against industry peers.
- Move from experimentation to a documented strategy with measurable production milestones.
- Close the skills gap as the top constraint on scaling.
- Establish governance over autonomous agents *before* deploying them at scale.
- Embed Trustworthy AI across seven dimensions.

## 4. Adoption Roadmap / Phases
Deloitte does not name discrete phases publicly the way McKinsey or Microsoft do, but its published guidance prescribes:
1. **Strategy and ambition setting** — explicit goals, value-at-stake, board-level sponsorship.
2. **Trustworthy AI foundation** — governance, ethics, resilience, privacy, security, legal, value alignment instrumented from day one.
3. **Use-case prioritization and pilot** — productivity wins plus differentiated bets.
4. **Production scaling** — operating model, MLOps / LLMOps, governance gates.
5. **Agentic AI readiness** — governance, monitoring, and accountability for autonomous agents.
6. **Continuous workforce reshape** — education at scale as the dominant lever.

## 5. Training & Workforce Enablement
- Education is the #1 talent strategy adjustment in the 2025 survey.
- Deloitte recommends an "AI Academy"-style universal upskilling, plus dedicated AI engineering tracks.
- Role redesign and prompt fluency are treated as core skills, not specialist skills.

## 6. Product Ideation & Development
- Pair AI-enabled productivity wins with differentiated "novel value" plays.
- Industry-specific accelerators (Deloitte publishes vertical playbooks).
- Increasing emphasis on agentic AI for end-to-end process redesign.

## 7. Cost Reduction Levers
- Knowledge-work productivity (drafting, summarization, code generation).
- Customer service automation with measurable containment.
- Back-office automation in finance, HR, procurement.
- Note: Deloitte's surveys consistently report productivity / efficiency as the **first** value captured; transformative top-line moves come later.

## 8. Portfolio & Competitive Positioning
- Deloitte positions Trustworthy AI as a competitive differentiator in regulated industries (financial services, healthcare, public sector, insurance).
- Their quarterly survey gives clients a credible benchmark against peers, reinforcing Deloitte's advisory positioning.

## 9. Market Expansion Plays
- AI-enabled diligence, sector reinvention, and customer-experience reinvention engagements.
- Sector academies (financial services AI Institute, health AI institute, etc.) drive cross-selling.
- Global footprint enables multi-country adoption rollouts.

## 10. Technology & Architecture
- Vendor-agnostic; deep relationships with Microsoft, AWS, Google, Oracle, Salesforce, NVIDIA, SAP.
- Recommends modular reference architecture with a governance layer overlaying data + model + application planes.
- Increasingly emphasizes agent orchestration platforms.

## 11. Governance, Risk & Responsible AI
**Trustworthy AI Framework** — seven dimensions: governance, ethics, resilience, privacy, security, legal / contractual obligations, alignment with company values. Deloitte explicitly extends this to **agentic AI** with new control points (autonomy boundaries, escalation patterns, audit trails). The framework is the centerpiece of Deloitte's regulated-industry positioning.

## 12. KPIs & Measured Outcomes
Headline survey findings (Deloitte State of GenAI / State of AI in the Enterprise 2025–2026):
- Worker access to AI **+50%** in 2025.
- Companies with **≥40%** of projects in production set to double in six months.
- **2x** as many leaders as the prior year report transformative impact.
- Only **1 in 5** companies has mature governance for autonomous AI agents.
- AI skills gap = #1 barrier to integration.

## 13. Risks, Constraints & Open Questions
- Governance lag is the dominant operational risk.
- Productivity-first deployments can plateau without reinvention.
- Sustained ROI measurement remains immature at most surveyed firms.
