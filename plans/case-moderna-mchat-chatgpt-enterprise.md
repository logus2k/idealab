# Moderna — mChat → ChatGPT Enterprise (750 GPTs)

## 1. Executive Snapshot
Moderna built **mChat** on the OpenAI API in early 2023 — an internal ChatGPT analogue that reached **>80% adoption** firm-wide. In 2024 it expanded to **ChatGPT Enterprise** for ~3,000 employees and, within two months, employees had created **750+ custom GPTs**, with the **average user holding 120 conversations per week** and **40% of users authoring their own GPTs**. AI is positioned as central to Moderna's ambition to bring **up to 15 new mRNA products to market within five years**.

## 2. Context & Strategic Drivers
Moderna's bet: an mRNA-platform company has more in common with a software company than with traditional pharma — meaning AI's productivity, research, and operations leverage is structural, not optional. Drug development cycles are long and expensive; AI is the lever that compresses them.

## 3. Objectives & Target Outcomes
- Make AI a universal capability — every employee has access.
- Compress R&D cycles for mRNA medicines.
- Accelerate non-R&D functions (legal, commercial, manufacturing).
- Build a culture of bottom-up AI tool creation (the 750-GPT marketplace).

## 4. Adoption Roadmap / Phases
1. **mChat launch** (early 2023) — internal ChatGPT built on OpenAI API, >80% adoption.
2. **Expanded OpenAI partnership** — ChatGPT Enterprise for ~3,000 employees.
3. **Custom GPT explosion** — 750 GPTs in 2 months across legal, research, manufacturing, commercial.
4. **Specialized agents** — Dose ID (clinical study data-analysis assistant).
5. **Ongoing R&D acceleration** — AI for mRNA design, clinical operations, regulatory writing.

## 5. Training & Workforce Enablement
- Self-service GPT marketplace creates organic learning loops.
- 40% of users author GPTs — a high creator-to-consumer ratio.
- AI fluency is treated as a base expectation across functions.

## 6. Product Ideation & Development
- AI accelerates mRNA design, target validation, candidate screening.
- Dose ID assists clinical study teams with data analysis.
- AI in regulatory writing and submissions compresses time-to-IND/BLA.
- 15 product launches in 5 years is the named ambition — AI is the leverage.

## 7. Cost Reduction Levers
- Compressed cycle times across R&D and clinical operations.
- Legal, commercial, and manufacturing productivity via custom GPTs.
- Reduced manual data analysis effort in clinical studies.

## 8. Portfolio & Competitive Positioning
- Moderna positions itself as the AI-native mRNA company — a structural differentiator vs. legacy big pharma.
- Strong OpenAI alliance gives privileged access to frontier capabilities.
- 750 GPTs is a public KPI of AI density that few peers match.

## 9. Market Expansion Plays
- Pipeline breadth — 15 products in 5 years spans vaccines (RSV, flu, COVID variants), individualized cancer treatments (mRNA neoantigen).
- AI-enabled localization for regulatory submissions across geographies.
- Faster time-to-market unlocks earlier revenue in new indications.

## 10. Technology & Architecture
- **OpenAI API** (originally) → **ChatGPT Enterprise** at scale.
- Custom GPTs built directly by employees on top of ChatGPT Enterprise.
- Integration with internal data and research systems for context-aware agents.

## 11. Governance, Risk & Responsible AI
- ChatGPT Enterprise's no-training-on-customer-data guarantee underpins regulated-data use.
- Clinical agents (Dose ID) must operate under FDA-relevant data governance.
- Human-in-the-loop standard for clinical and regulatory outputs.

## 12. KPIs & Measured Outcomes
- **80%+** adoption of mChat firm-wide.
- **~3,000** ChatGPT Enterprise users.
- **750+** custom GPTs in two months.
- **120** average conversations per user per week.
- **40%** of users created their own GPTs.
- Ambition: **15** new products in 5 years.

## 13. Risks, Constraints & Open Questions
- Regulatory exposure: agents touching clinical data must meet FDA / EMA standards.
- IP protection in an OpenAI-first stack requires careful contracting.
- Sustaining adoption at 120 conversations/week/user requires ongoing tooling investment.
