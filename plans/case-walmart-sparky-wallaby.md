# Walmart — Sparky, Wally + Wallaby LLM (Agentic Retail AI)

## 1. Executive Snapshot
Walmart has rolled out a portfolio of generative-AI assistants on top of its own retail-tuned LLM, **Wallaby**: **Sparky** (customer-facing agentic shopping assistant in the mobile app, launched June 2025), **Wally** (merchant assistant, debuted March 2025), and other internal copilots for store managers and associates. Sparky moves shopping from keyword search to a conversational, multimodal experience and is positioned as one of four AI "super agents" that handle customer, merchant, store associate, and developer workflows.

## 2. Context & Strategic Drivers
Walmart's strategic premise: agentic AI rewires the shopping interface from search-and-click to dialogue, and the retailer that owns that interface owns the customer relationship. Owning Wallaby (a retail-context LLM) avoids generic-assistant feel and protects proprietary retail data.

## 3. Objectives & Target Outcomes
- Make conversational, multimodal shopping the default in the Walmart app.
- Equip merchants with GenAI tools to manage assortment, suppliers, and pricing faster.
- Empower store associates with copilots for tasks (My Assistant pattern).
- Build a four-agent architecture (customer, merchant, associate, developer).

## 4. Adoption Roadmap / Phases
1. **Foundational ML investments** in search, recommendations, supply chain (pre-2024).
2. **My Assistant** (internal GenAI for corporate associates) — first wave.
3. **Wally** for merchants (March 2025) — supplier negotiation, assortment, pricing support.
4. **Sparky** for customers (June 2025) — conversational shopping in the app.
5. **Wallaby LLM** as retail-specific foundation model providing context to all agents.
6. **Four super agents** vision: customer, merchant, store, developer.
7. **Sparky expansion** to reordering, service booking, multimodal (text / image / audio / video).

## 5. Training & Workforce Enablement
- Internal copilots (My Assistant) are themselves a training mechanism — associates learn AI by using AI.
- Store-manager and merchant copilots reshape role design.
- Developer-facing agents accelerate the engineering workforce.

## 6. Product Ideation & Development
- Sparky reinvents the digital storefront as a conversation.
- Wally redefines merchant workflows for assortment and supplier interaction.
- Wallaby LLM provides retail-tuned context that generic models cannot — a strategic IP asset.
- Multimodal capabilities (image, video) extend product discovery.

## 7. Cost Reduction Levers
- Customer service deflection through Sparky.
- Merchant productivity via Wally (faster decisions, less manual prep).
- Store associate productivity through internal copilots.
- Software engineering productivity through developer agent.

## 8. Portfolio & Competitive Positioning
- Sparky positions Walmart as the agentic-shopping leader vs. Amazon Rufus, Target's AI features, and others.
- Wallaby LLM is the technical differentiator: retail-tuned beats generic.
- Four-agent architecture is a published bet on agentic-AI dominance in retail.

## 9. Market Expansion Plays
- Conversational commerce expands engagement across customer segments (incl. less digitally-native shoppers).
- Multimodal inputs broaden product discovery scenarios (e.g., upload a photo).
- Service booking opens new revenue lines beyond goods.

## 10. Technology & Architecture
- **Wallaby LLM** — Walmart-trained / fine-tuned retail foundation model.
- **Sparky** customer agent in the Walmart mobile app.
- **Wally** merchant agent in internal tooling.
- Multimodal input / output stack (text, image, audio, video).
- Cloud + edge architecture across stores and digital channels.

## 11. Governance, Risk & Responsible AI
- Walmart publishes AI principles and content guardrails for customer-facing AI.
- Human review remains for high-stakes recommendations and operational decisions.
- Brand-safety guardrails important for the merchant tooling (Wally) interacting with third parties.

## 12. KPIs & Measured Outcomes
- Sparky live across all product categories in the Walmart mobile app.
- Multiple agent surfaces in production simultaneously (customer, merchant, store, developer).
- Walmart frames the architecture as four "super agents" — a public KPI of agentic breadth.
- Adjacent benchmarks (TRN Digital reports on retail Copilot deployments): +34% online revenue, -22% cart abandonment, +18% CSAT (industry pattern, not Walmart-specific).

## 13. Risks, Constraints & Open Questions
- Agentic shopping requires sustained trust — hallucinations or wrong recommendations directly hit revenue.
- Wallaby's data scope must avoid privacy missteps as multimodal inputs grow.
- Coordination across four agent surfaces is itself a complex governance problem.
