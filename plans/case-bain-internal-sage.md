# Bain & Company (Internal) — Sage + ChatGPT Enterprise Firm-wide

## 1. Executive Snapshot
Bain runs its own AI on itself, at scale: **Sage**, Bain's proprietary information-agent platform powered by **OpenAI models** over Bain's accumulated knowledge corpus; firm-wide **ChatGPT Enterprise** access for **19,000 people across 67 cities** through the **OpenAI strategic alliance** (formed Feb 2023); a **MyGPT marketplace** with **4,000 customized MyGPTs**; plus integration with **Microsoft Copilot**, ZoomAI, and daily tools. Tech and AI-enabled revenue reached **30% of 2024 business** and is projected to exceed **50%** — AI is now central to **25%+ of the firm's work** (Bain press release, Dec 10 2024).

## 2. Context & Strategic Drivers
Bain's drivers: (a) demonstrate AI-on-AI credibility to clients; (b) reduce per-engagement effort by surfacing prior analysis and benchmarks; (c) sustain a proprietary knowledge moat that off-the-shelf tools can't replicate; (d) anchor the OpenAI / Microsoft alliances by being the lighthouse adopter.

## 3. Objectives & Target Outcomes
- Make AI a universal capability for every consultant.
- Surface decades of proprietary insights instantly via Sage.
- Encourage bottom-up tool creation through MyGPT marketplace.
- Strengthen client offer credibility by being the AI-on-AI exemplar.

## 4. Adoption Roadmap / Phases
1. **OpenAI strategic alliance** formalized (Feb 2023).
2. **Sage deployment** — proprietary information-agent platform powered by OpenAI models over Bain's knowledge corpus.
3. **ChatGPT Enterprise** rolled out across 19,000 people in 67 cities.
4. **MyGPT marketplace** — 4,000 customized MyGPTs built by teams.
5. **Microsoft Copilot + ZoomAI integration** within daily productivity tools.
6. **Digital practices restructure** (Dec 10 2024) — unified leadership over AI, Insights & Solutions + Enterprise Technology (Chuck Whitten global; Sarah Elk Americas; Florian Mueller EMEA; Richard Fleming APAC; Stuart Sim leading 1,500+ AI and engineering delivery; Roy Singh on AI strategy; Miles Cook on alliances).
7. **Expanded partnerships** — OpenAI, Microsoft, AWS, Google, SAP, Salesforce, IBM, plus Palantir, AI Aspire (Andrew Ng), and OpenAI Deployment Company investment (2026).

## 5. Training & Workforce Enablement
- AI fluency uplift for 19,000 Bain employees across 67 cities.
- MyGPT marketplace is itself a learning mechanism — consultants learn by building.
- Career-track integration: AI-enabled ways of working embedded in case-team norms.
- 1,500+ dedicated AI and engineering personnel anchor the global delivery model.

## 6. Product Ideation & Development
- Sage serves as both internal knowledge accelerator and demo asset for clients.
- Internal GPTs templated into client-ready offerings.
- Bain's "AI, Insights, and Solutions" practice (digital practice expansion) drives productization.

## 7. Cost Reduction Levers
- Knowledge reuse — Sage eliminates rework of foundational decks and benchmarks.
- AI-augmented case teams reduce per-engagement effort.
- Faster industry research and synthesis.
- Reduced internal training overhead via self-service marketplace.

## 8. Portfolio & Competitive Positioning
- Sage is a structural differentiator vs. McKinsey QuantumBlack and BCG X.
- OpenAI alliance + investment in OpenAI Deployment Company is a sustained moat.
- Tech and AI-enabled revenue: **30% of 2024 business**, projected to exceed **50%** within years.
- AI central to **25%+** of the firm's work (Dec 10 2024 press release).

## 9. Market Expansion Plays
- Private-equity portfolio plays — AI value creation rolled across portfolio companies.
- New regional advisory engagements aligned to OpenAI / Microsoft deployment.
- AI-Aspire (Andrew Ng) partnership expands technical depth on AI implementations.

## 10. Technology & Architecture
- **Sage** — Bain's proprietary information-agent platform powered by OpenAI models, delivering insights from accumulated firm data instantaneously.
- **ChatGPT Enterprise** firm-wide.
- **Microsoft Copilot** and **ZoomAI** integrated with daily productivity tools.
- Sentiment-analysis applications and other in-house tooling.
- **Alliance ecosystem**: OpenAI, Microsoft, AWS, Google, SAP, Salesforce, IBM, Palantir, AI Aspire — 700+ partner organizations in total.

## 11. Governance, Risk & Responsible AI
- Codified **Responsible AI Use Policy**.
- Participation in Microsoft's Responsible AI Partner Initiative.
- Human-in-the-loop emphasis across published material.
- Security and safety guardrails built into deployment lifecycle.

## 12. KPIs & Measured Outcomes
- **19,000 people across 67 cities** on AI tools (universal).
- **4,000 customized MyGPTs** built by teams.
- **1,500+** AI and engineering personnel in global delivery.
- **700+** partner organizations in the ecosystem.
- **30%** of 2024 revenue from tech & AI, projected **>50%** within years.
- AI **central to 25%+** of the firm's work.
- (All figures from the Dec 10 2024 Bain press release; OpenAI alliance reportedly underpins multi-billion-dollar joint client work over its multi-year span.)

## 13. Risks, Constraints & Open Questions
- Heavy OpenAI / Microsoft concentration reduces stack diversity.
- Internal proprietary corpus quality determines Sage's quality — knowledge-management investment must remain ongoing.
- Pricing parity with peers may shift as competing firms (McKinsey, BCG, Accenture) scale their own internal AI.
