# Gartner — AI Maturity Model + AI TRiSM + CIO Roadmap

## 1. Executive Snapshot
Gartner's framework comprises three connected artifacts: (a) the **AI Maturity Model** (five stages — Foundational, Emerging, Operational, Scaled, Transformational); (b) the **AI Roadmap toolkit** organizing AI delivery into **seven workstreams** for CIOs; and (c) **AI TRiSM (Trust, Risk and Security Management)** — the named governance discipline Gartner expects most enterprises will implement within five years.

## 2. Context & Strategic Drivers
Gartner's premise: CIOs are now the de-facto orchestrators of AI value, accountable for operational efficiency, growth, and profitability simultaneously. The **2025 Hype Cycle for AI** marks a shift from "Flash to Function" — agents, ModelOps, synthetic data, and AI engineering replace hype-stage entrants. Gartner research finds **45% of organizations with high AI maturity keep AI projects operational for at least three years**, vs. far lower retention at low-maturity firms.

## 3. Objectives & Target Outcomes
- Diagnose where the enterprise sits today against the five-stage maturity ladder.
- Pick and sequence priorities across the seven workstreams of the AI Roadmap.
- Implement AI TRiSM controls before scaling.
- Move toward Scaled / Transformational maturity, where projects survive multi-year.

## 4. Adoption Roadmap / Phases
**AI Maturity Model — five stages:**
1. **Foundational** — ad hoc experimentation, limited coordination.
2. **Emerging** — early pilots, growing executive interest.
3. **Operational** — AI embedded in select processes, defined ownership.
4. **Scaled** — AI capabilities deployed across functions with measurable ROI.
5. **Transformational** — AI reshapes decision making, operating models, and competitive advantage.

**AI Roadmap — seven workstreams** that CIOs sequence based on maturity and goals (named workstreams include strategy, data, AI engineering / MLOps, talent, governance / TRiSM, operating model, and adoption — Gartner reserves the toolkit's exact wording behind paywall, so consultant should pull the canonical seven names from the toolkit when accessible).

## 5. Training & Workforce Enablement
- Gartner places talent as one of the seven workstreams.
- Recommends a balance of AI engineering specialists and broad workforce AI literacy.
- CIOs called out as "value orchestrators" — a named role shift.

## 6. Product Ideation & Development
- Gartner's Hype Cycle for AI guides where to invest based on maturity vs. risk.
- 2025 Hype Cycle highlights: agentic AI, ModelOps, synthetic data, AI engineering as the high-impact innovations breaking through.
- AI TRiSM and Responsible AI flagged as prerequisites for scaling.

## 7. Cost Reduction Levers
- AI engineering / MLOps discipline as the primary lever to reduce per-model TCO.
- Synthetic data to lower data-acquisition cost.
- Agent-based automation across functional silos.

## 8. Portfolio & Competitive Positioning
Gartner positions itself as the neutral measurement layer — clients use the Maturity Model and Hype Cycle to benchmark and decide vendor / partner choices. The framework is intentionally vendor-agnostic.

## 9. Market Expansion Plays
- AI-driven customer-experience reinvention.
- Decision intelligence (AI-enabled decisions at scale).
- Industry-specific use cases that Gartner tracks in its sector Hype Cycles.

## 10. Technology & Architecture
- Gartner doesn't prescribe a stack; it categorizes technologies by maturity and risk.
- AI engineering and ModelOps as core architectural disciplines.
- AI TRiSM tools (model monitoring, content safety, drift, governance) as a recognized category.

## 11. Governance, Risk & Responsible AI
**AI TRiSM** is Gartner's flagship governance framework — Trust, Risk, and Security Management for AI. Components include model risk management, AI governance, data privacy in AI, prompt-injection / adversarial defense, model monitoring, and content safety. Gartner flags AI TRiSM as dominating the **Peak of Inflated Expectations** for AI innovations expected to reach mainstream adoption within five years.

## 12. KPIs & Measured Outcomes
- **45%** of orgs with high AI maturity keep AI projects operational for ≥3 years (Gartner, June 2025).
- Hype Cycle 2025 shift "from Flash to Function" identifies agentic AI, ModelOps, synthetic data, AI engineering as the mainstream-bound innovations.
- The Maturity Model assigns measurable rubrics per stage (paywall content — consultant should source exact metrics from a current Gartner subscription).

## 13. Risks, Constraints & Open Questions
- The toolkit's deepest content sits behind Gartner subscription paywalls.
- Hype Cycle positioning shifts annually; consultant must keep up.
- AI TRiSM is a recognized discipline but vendor implementation is fragmented.
